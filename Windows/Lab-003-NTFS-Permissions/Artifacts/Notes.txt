Lab Notes

- NTFS permissions determine access to files and folders.
- Ownership is separate from permissions.
- Users inherit permissions through group membership.
- Explicit Deny overrides Allow.
- Effective permissions combine all applicable Allow permissions.
- Inherited permissions come from parent folders.
- Standard users should receive only the permissions required to perform their job.
- The Effective Access tool can calculate a user's actual permissions.
- Read & Execute allows viewing files but prevents modifying or deleting them.
